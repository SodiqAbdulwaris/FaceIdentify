## What changed

- **The development profile can process a video.** The video path takes one worker turn per batch
  of frames (`client.turn()`), and the development profile's perception had none, so any video
  processed under it failed. The profile runs in the process, so its turn is a no-op
  (`DevelopmentPerception.turn`, `backend/api/development.py`). New
  `test_the_development_host_processes_a_video_and_finds_a_known_face_in_it`: the host processes a
  picture and then a clip whose frame it is, and the clip shows that person once, over time. It fails
  without the turn (mutation run).
- **The desktop video run** (`frontend/e2e/video.e2e.tsx`): the real web app against the real
  backend started as the shell starts it. A synthetic one-colour clip (an hour of 64 x 48 video,
  made by the pinned ffmpeg) and one of its frames, extracted by the same ffmpeg, are imported through
  the stood-in picker. The frame is processed first (a video never creates a person); then the video
  is processed, paused from its screen, resumed and finished, and the frame's person appears under
  Appearances from 0:00. No real face is used or committed.
- **CI:** the desktop job installs the pinned ffmpeg as the backend job does (cached by its SHA-256),
  so `npm run test:e2e` runs the video test there.
